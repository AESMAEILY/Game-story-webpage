Story & lore

Fleeing a homeland torn by clan warfare, Viking raider Eivor leads their clan across the sea to settle in England, forging alliances and rivalries with Saxon kings and rival Norse settlers alike. Beneath the raids and politics, an ancient conflict between the Hidden Ones and the Order of the Ancients resurfaces, pulling Eivor into a war that predates the Vikings by centuries.
