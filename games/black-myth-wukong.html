Story & lore

Long after the Monkey King renounced his title and vanished, a nameless wandering monk called the Destined One sets out across a fractured mythic China to recover the six relics of his shattered power. Drawing directly from the classic Journey to the West, the road puts him against the same demons, gods, and old grudges that once tested the legend himself.
