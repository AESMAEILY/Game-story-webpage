Story & lore

Signing up for Tom Nook's deserted island getaway package, a lone villager arrives with nothing but a tent and starts turning an empty island into a working town, one bridge, museum donation, and neighbor at a time. There's no real ending, only seasons and holidays looping year over year while the island, and whoever moves in beside you, keeps changing shape.
