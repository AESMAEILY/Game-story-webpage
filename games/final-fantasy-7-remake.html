Story & lore

Mercenary Cloud Strife takes a job with the eco-terrorist group AVALANCHE, bombing the mako reactors draining the dying planet beneath the plate-city of Midgar. As Shinra's retaliation escalates and a mysterious flower girl named Aerith is pulled into the fight, Cloud's fragmented memories of his past start bleeding into a present that seems to be rewriting itself around him.
