Story & lore

Long after burning his way through the Greek pantheon, Kratos now lives quietly in the Norse realm of Midgard, until his wife Faye's death and dying wish send him and his son Atreus on a journey to scatter her ashes from the highest peak in all the realms. The trip drags a god trying to leave violence behind into the middle of Norse mythology's own coming apocalypse, with fatherhood as the real, harder fight underneath it.
