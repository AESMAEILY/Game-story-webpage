Story & lore

Rebuilt on Valve's Source 2 engine, Counter-Strike 2 carries forward the series' long-running, story-light standoff between a Counter-Terrorist unit and a Terrorist faction fighting over hostages and bomb sites on maps drawn from two decades of competitive play. There's no campaign to speak of: the drama is generated round by round, economy by economy, by whoever's on the server with you.
