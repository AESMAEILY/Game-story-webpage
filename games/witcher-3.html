Story & lore

With the Northern Kingdoms at war with Nilfgaard, monster hunter Geralt of Rivia searches for Ciri, the adopted daughter he trained as a witcher, before the spectral Wild Hunt finds her first. The search for Ciri weaves through a continent's worth of local tragedies and political schemes, with Geralt's choices along the way shaping just how much of it he can actually save.
