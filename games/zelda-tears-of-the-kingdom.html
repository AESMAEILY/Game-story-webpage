Story & lore

A corrupted secret beneath Hyrule Castle awakens an ancient evil and flings Link into the sky islands above, stripping Zelda from his side and scattering the kingdom's history across ruins on the surface, sky, and a newly opened Depths below. Piecing together dragon tears of memory, Link has to rebuild Hyrule's shattered alliances before Ganondorf's return finishes what the corruption started.

The Upheaval Beneath Hyrule Castle

Years after the Calamity's defeat, Link and Princess Zelda descend into a gloom-filled cavern beneath Hyrule Castle, following Purah's research into a mysterious illness spreading across the land. There they find ancient murals depicting Hyrule's founding and a forgotten conflict called the Imprisoning War, fought against a figure known only as the Demon King. Investigating further, they disturb a withered, mummified corpse chained in the depths, which stirs to life and violently attacks them. The battle shatters the Master Sword and corrupts Link's right arm with gloom, and the ground gives way entirely, sending Hyrule Castle rocketing into the sky while Zelda plummets into the chasm below, disappearing with a strange glowing stone in hand.

A New Arm and a Fractured Kingdom

A disembodied, glowing arm catches Link as he falls and carries him to the Great Sky Island, where he awakens with his ruined arm replaced by this mysterious Zonai limb. He meets the spirit of Rauru, an ancient Zonai king who explains that the arm grants powerful new abilities: Ultrahand, Fuse, Ascend, and Recall. Descending back to Hyrule, Link finds the kingdom in chaos: sky islands have appeared overhead, deep chasms leading to a subterranean realm called the Depths have opened across the surface, and monsters empowered by gloom roam freely while Zelda remains missing.

The Regional Sages and the Secret Stones

Link reunites with old allies turned regional leaders — Sidon of the Zora, Tulin of the Rito, Yunobo of the Gorons, and Riju of the Gerudo — each contending with gloom-corrupted disasters threatening their homelands, orchestrated by shadowy agents of Ganondorf. By clearing ancient Zonai temples and defeating powerful bosses, Link helps each ally awaken a secret stone and the spirit of an ancient sage bound to it, granting them sage powers and cementing them as Zelda's chosen champions in the fight to come, alongside the mechanical construct Mineru, a fifth sage recovered from the Depths.

Zelda's Journey to the Past

Piecing together visions called 'dragon's tears,' Link learns the devastating truth: the Zelda he occasionally glimpses at Hyrule Castle is a phantom illusion conjured by Ganondorf's servant. The real Zelda fell through time via her secret stone into Hyrule's founding era, meeting King Rauru and Queen Sonia, and Ganondorf himself, then the ambitious Gerudo King who cloaked his true nature. After Ganondorf murdered Sonia and rebelled, Rauru and the original sages sealed him beneath the castle in the Imprisoning War, but the seal's inevitable failure led Zelda to a desperate act: she transformed herself into the eternal Light Dragon, sacrificing her humanity to keep the reforged Master Sword bathed in restorative light for the ten millennia until Link could wield it again.

The Demon King's Return

The mummified figure Link and Zelda disturbed was Ganondorf himself, freed from his ancient seal and now hunting the secret stones to seize Zonai power and reshape Hyrule as his own. With the Master Sword restored and his allied sages at his side, Link infiltrates the Depths and battles his way to Ganondorf beneath Hyrule Castle, ultimately facing him amid the ruins of the flying fortress in the sky.

Demon Dragon and the True Ending

As Link overwhelms him, Ganondorf swallows his own secret stone in a last desperate gambit, transforming into the monstrous, world-threatening Demon Dragon. The Light Dragon — Zelda in her transformed state — arrives to aid Link, letting him ride her and strike the corrupted stone directly, destroying Ganondorf for good and ending the threat. With Ganondorf's malice purged, Rauru and Sonia's lingering spirits use their remaining power to restore Zelda to human form and heal Link's arm before finally passing on. Link and Zelda reunite on the surface with their allies, Mineru bids the pair farewell, and the newly empowered regional sages vow to safeguard their lands, leaving Hyrule battered but at peace as the kingdom begins to rebuild.
