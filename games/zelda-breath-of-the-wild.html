Story & lore

A hundred years after Calamity Ganon's return laid waste to Hyrule and its Guardian army, Link wakes from a century of healing sleep with almost no memory of what happened. Guided by a spirit calling herself Zelda, he sets out across an open, ruined kingdom to free the four Divine Beasts and finally put an end to the calamity he apparently failed to stop the first time.
