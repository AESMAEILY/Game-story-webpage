Story & lore

Years after reconnecting America, Sam Porter Bridges is pulled out of quiet retirement for a new delivery network stretching across Mexico and beyond, as the mysterious organization Drawbridge pushes an agenda that puts him at odds with the peace he helped build. Otherworldly Beached Things, fractured nations, and old ghosts from the first Death Stranding all resurface as Sam carries the world's last threads of connection on his back.
